# The Study (study)
- Direction: The homepage as the opening pages of a beautifully set clinical text, on warm cream, with gilt used the way a fine press uses it.
- Techniques: Fraunces as a book face at a 62ch measure, drop caps with letter-spaced uppercase lead-ins, running head that follows the chapter, gilt ribbon bookmark tracking scroll progress, contents page with dot leaders and folios, real numbered footnotes at the foot of each chapter with return links, marginalia that sit in the left margin above 1180px and collapse to run-in keys below, screenshots set as Plates I to IV, catchwords at the foot of every page, SVG fleurons and rules, laid-paper noise tile as a data URI, a print stylesheet that paginates the page as a book, no cards and no buttons.
- Build: v1 ships the whole page as front matter plus five chapters plus a colophon: frontispiece, contents, I The Principle, II The Product with four plates, III The Philosophy, IV The Founder, V Free tools, closing and footer. All copy from the bank, all four stats carried as footnotes, both free tools linked as plain links, one h1, skip link, focus-visible rules, complete prefers-reduced-motion variant that shows the finished composition.
- Pass 1: Ribbon given real presence (17px woven-silk gradient with dark selvedges, drop shadows, deeper notched tail); all scroll fade-ins deleted so every chapter arrives as set type, leaving only the title-page settle, now tightened to finish inside 850ms; plates complexified with a tipped-in plate leaf, an outer printed rule plus an inner plate-mark impression, a printer's rule beside each plate number, and small-cap captions under an Oxford rule.
- Pass 2: Catchwords carried through to the foot of six pages (Everything / Notes. / Therapy / Alembic / Two / It), each the first word overleaf, set right of a centred folio on a baseline-aligned grid; a full print stylesheet added (page margins, no bars or ribbon, a static running head printed at the head of every chapter from data-runhead, chapters breaking to new pages, plates, footnotes and feet kept whole, title page exempt); the running head script now reads its labels from the same data-runhead attributes so screen and print can never drift, with the observer band retuned to -40/-55; frontispiece settle cut from a five-step stagger to one 440ms arrival.
- Pass 3: Three defects hunted and fixed. The contents and tools leaders jittered, because a dotted border phases from the left, so every row's last dot stopped a different distance from its folio; they are now a right anchored dot pattern on the baseline, so the leaders line up down the page and always end flush at the number. The footnote reference marks sat a hair loose from the punctuation they follow and could wrap onto a line alone; they are kerned back and each is bound to its word in a nowrap span. Letter spaced type carries a trailing space after its last letter, which pushed every centred small cap line left of true centre, left the right aligned marginal keys and portrait caption short of their column edge, and ended the running head nav short of the margin the wordmark starts at; all seven are now balanced with an equal lead or pulled back. One inline style removed with them.
- Weight: 544.5 KB total transfer (index.html 48.0 KB, four loaded plates 302.8 KB, headshot 193.8 KB). Folder is 51.3 KB across two files, no vendored libraries. Fonts, favicon and the 120 KB mark are exempt.
