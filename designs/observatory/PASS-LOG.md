# The Observatory (observatory)
- Direction: the dark-glass direction made vast and cinematic, with a live shader sky as the ground of the whole page and typography floating at different apparent distances above it.
- Techniques: raw WebGL fragment shader (no libraries) with domain-warped fbm, half-resolution render target upscaled by the compositor, 30 fps cap, devicePixelRatio capped at 2, pixel budget plus one-shot adaptive downscale, painted gradient fallback for reduced motion and no-WebGL, scroll depth on the individual `translate` property so it composes with the entry `transform`, IntersectionObserver reveals with a depth-of-field blur on headings, pointer-tracked specular glint on glass panes, holding veil for guaranteed text contrast.
- Build: v1 shipped the full homepage (nav, hero, principle, product, philosophy, founder, free tools, closing, footer) staged as strata over the shader sky; parallax and live backdrop blur stand down under 900 px, and the whole page has a complete static composition under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Pass 1: Fixed the 75px phone overflow (`.stratum::before` resolved to 525px wide via `width:min(1200px,140%)` on six sections, plus a 6px bleed from `.hero-sub::before`); added `overflow-x:clip` on html and body; cut reveal timing so copy is legible about 400ms after entering the viewport and compressed the hero ladder from 1.35s to 0.62s.
- Pass 2: Added a four-period weather drift (240s, 367s, 422s, 304s) plus a random per-load phase, handed to the shader as one CPU-computed vec4 so the per-pixel cost is a register read and the octave count is unchanged; graded the strata into three tiers (far: principle panes and stats rail; mid: product, tools, founder, philosophy body; near: screenshot viewer and pull quote) in parallax rate, pane opacity, border and shadow; the scroll cue now retires on first scroll and never returns; and dropped the painted fallback's gold radial from 0.20 to 0.13 after the maths showed its hotspot put body copy at 4.31:1.
- Pass 3: Collapsed the 861 to 900px seam where the trio showed its desktop stair wearing the phone's flat pane chrome with no depth grading, so trio columns, tier chrome, backdrop blur and the parallax gate now all switch at one 900/901 boundary; gave the skip link a dark inset focus ring after an audit found the global pale green ring measured 1.00:1 against its own pale green chip; normalised uppercase micro-label tracking (cue 0.24em to 0.22em to match the kicker it echoes, viewer name 0.10em to 0.14em, footer nav 0.08em to 0.13em); added text-wrap:pretty to the prose that sits on bare sky.
- Weight (final): 562 KB counted (index.html 56 KB, four app screenshots 311 KB, founder headshot 194 KB). Fonts, fonts.css, favicon, apple-touch-icon and the 120 KB mark are excluded per the brief; including them the page is 754 KB.
