# The Filing Room (filing-room)
- Direction: "Everything is a file" made physical, a deep green-black room of cream paper records with small brass details.
- Techniques: procedural SVG feTurbulence paper grain (data URI, multiply-blended), stacked box-shadow leaf edges for believable paper thickness, one consistent top-left light for every shadow, CSS-only open filing drawer with staggered index tabs, details/summary folder that opens and closes, ARIA tablist hanging file of mounted documents with a no-JS stacked fallback, SVG brass paperclip, IntersectionObserver reveals using transform and opacity only, no rAF and no canvas.
- Build: v1 shipped the full homepage staged as a room of records. Sticky brass rail with a compact Index disclosure under 1020 px, hero as an open drawer of six labelled folders with the A mark as an embossed seal on the top file, the Principle as a manila folder that opens and closes with three punched leaves inside, the four stats as brass-framed label plates hung on a hairline rail, the six product features as staggered index-tab cards, the screenshot strip as a hanging file with four index tabs, the philosophy as a ruled foolscap sheet beside a dark pull quote, the founder as a typed record card next to a paper-clipped photograph, the free tools as two pull-out folders, and the closing as a stamped panel.
- Pass 1: Closed the dark void above the footer with a skirting board (brass hairline returning, floor gradient, footer border folded into it) and tightened the closing section's bottom padding; complexified the six product cards with a typed catalogue mark per card (Cabinet 1 Drawer 1 style) and an index tab that lights a gold hairline and darkens its label on hover or focus-within; cut reveal timing so nothing sits dim, opacity now 280ms with stagger capped at 90ms (was 720ms with stagger to 240ms).
- Pass 2: The hero drawer is now the index it looked like, six real anchor links to per-card ids with focus-visible rings on their tabs, a 47px tab hit area that changed no pixels of the drawing, lift on hover and focus gated behind no-preference, aria-hidden moved off the drawer and onto only the decorative face, stamp and drawer front, plus scroll-margin-top on every target and gated smooth scrolling; the philosophy foolscap and the founder record card gained two further leaves peeking down and right under the same light and a deeper resting shadow; the closing date stamp now lands at half a degree at phone width too; verified the tablist and Index disclosure by running the page's real script against a DOM harness, 15 of 15 behavioural checks pass (roving tabindex, arrows with wrap, Home, End, click, Escape closing and returning focus to the summary, link dismissal).
- Pass 3: Five hunted defects fixed. Two headings dropped a single word onto the last line (Software in service of / attention., Alembic arrives in / 2026.), so headings now balance and paragraphs wrap pretty, and the closing headline was widened from 16ch to 24ch to hold one line. The brass drawer pull was the one object in the room casting straight down (0 2px 6px) while everything else casts down and right, now 2px 3px 7px. Free tools sat on a doubled beat, its own 52px margin-top stacked on the 58px section-head margin for 110px where every other section uses 58px, so the redundant margin is gone. The footer copy of the label Get in touch went to a mailto while the header and compact index copies went to #contact, all three now resolve to the same place. The closing panel clipped its own contents, so the Say hello focus ring had a hard edge waiting for it, overflow hidden removed.
- Weight: 556 KB total transfer, 0.54 MB of the 2.5 MB budget (index.html 59 KB, four loaded screenshots 303 KB, founder headshot 194 KB; shared fonts, favicon and the A mark excluded per the brief).
