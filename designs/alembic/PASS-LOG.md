# The Alembic (alembic)
- Direction: the name taken literally, a working still whose distillation runs the length of the page and turns raw material into the permanent record.
- Techniques: hand-written canvas 2D simulation (no libraries), boiling charge with rising bubbles, curl-advected vapour, condensation with growth/pinning/coalescence/sliding on the condenser wall, 1D height-field liquid surfaces with impact ripples, offset-polyline glass rendering cached into sprites, document-space canvas locked to DOM measurement slots, scroll progress driving liquid levels and vapour flux.
- Build: v1 ships the full apparatus (boiling flask, swan neck, jacketed condenser rail with ground-glass joints at every stage boundary, delivery bend, receiving flask) spanning the whole document, with all seven required content stages staged as distillation stages I to VII, a pre-warmed simulation so the still is already running on arrival, and a deterministic static composition under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Pass 1: phone nav rebuilt as a proper aria disclosure panel (Menu button, no clipped labels, no edge mask); reveal transitions cut from 800ms to 340ms with delays capped at 150ms and the observer now firing at threshold 0.01; on phones the receiving flask is right-aligned so the delivery bend threads the gutter beside the Say hello button.
- Pass 2: menu panel given a solid #141715 ground so nothing ghosts through; a press on the hero flask now stirs the charge (bubble burst plus a real surface ripple, skipped under reduced motion, canvas still inert); the flask level and the receiver fill were retimed onto their own local scroll curves so the charge visibly depletes while the flask is on screen and the distillate lands full at the closing; focus-visible no longer overrides element border radius, and gold buttons get a dark inner ring plus a cream halo.
- Pass 3: text-wrap balance and pretty added, killing the single stranded word on three h2 last lines; nav disclosure breakpoint raised 760 to 960 because the inline row did not fit between 760 and about 880 and truncated exactly as the phone did, plus padding-block on the nav so overflow-x can never clip a focus ring; stage padding made asymmetric so consecutive sections stop doubling their beats (284px void at desktop down to 176px); the long stage label given nowrap so it can never wrap where the other six do not.
- Weight: 631 KB total (index.html 74 KB, PASS-LOG 2 KB + four screenshots 303 KB + headshot 194 KB + apple-touch-icon 59 KB; fonts, fonts.css, favicon and the 120 KB mark excluded per brief)
