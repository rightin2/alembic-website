This is a writing tool for clinicians. It helps you word the Risk section of a case note whose content you have already decided. It does not assess anyone, does not calculate or suggest a risk level, and does not tell you what to do. It is not a self-assessment and it is not a crisis service: it does not receive, read or respond to anything you type.

It replaces no form. If your service mandates a risk assessment form or proforma, this composes wording for your clinical note and does not stand in for that form, or for any screening tool your service requires.
